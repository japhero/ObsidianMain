## Input output characteristics of PD's
- They are nonlinear and the point at which there is a 1db attenuation from the input power to the PD we call it the saturation power. 
- Linear output of the system is necessary for some outputs such as ADC's and optoelectronic oscillators. 


- The IMD3 signal is a noise signal that lies close to the frequency of the input signal and degrades its performance because it is very difficult to filter frequencies close to the signal frequency. 
- The OIP3 is defined as an extrapolation point where the output RF power of the fundamental tone meets the IMD3 power
