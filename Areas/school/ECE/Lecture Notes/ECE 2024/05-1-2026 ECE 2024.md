### Rectifiers
$$
\begin{align}\underbrace{
C=\frac{I_{L}}{V_{r}}}_{\mathrm{half \space bridge}} &  &  
\underbrace{
C=\frac{I_{L}}{2V_{r}}}_{\mathrm{full \space bridge}}
\end{align}
$$
> $V_{r}$ is the ripple current where its the difference between the charged voltage and lowest discharge voltage before charging happens again.
