### Existence and Uniqueness for first order linear systems

> [!definition]
> Consider the initial value problem 
> $$
> \begin{align}
> y'(t)=P(t) \mathbf{y(t)}+\mathbf{g(t)}, &  & \mathbf{y}(t_{0})= \mathbf{y}_{0},
> \end{align}
> $$
> Where $y(t),P(t),g(t)$ and $y_{0}$ are defined as vectors and matrices respectively representing a first order linear system. Let the $n^2$ components of $P(t)$ and the $n$ components of $g(t)$ be continuous on the interval $(a,b),$ and let $t_{0}$ be in $(a,b).$ Then the initial value problem has a unique solution that exists on the entire interval $(a,b)$.
