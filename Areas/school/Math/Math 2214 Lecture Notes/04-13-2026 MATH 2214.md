## Section 4.6 Complex Eigen values 

- Complex eigen values always occur in conjugate pairs.
- If $(\lambda,\mathbf{x})$ is an eigenpair, then $(\bar{\lambda}, \bar{\mathbf{x}})$ is another eigenpair.

**Theorem**
if $y(t)-u(t)+iv(t)$ is a complex valued solution of $y'=Ay$ where t is $(-\infty,\infty)$ then $u(t)$ and $v(t)$ is each a solution of this system.

## Algebraic vs Geometric Multiplicity  
- algabraic multiplicity is the amount of times that we see the eigenvalue within the characteristic polynomial. 
- Geometric multiplicity is the amount of L.I eigen vectors per eigenvalue 
- Generally G.M < A.M


## Section 4.8 Non Homogenous Linear systems

- Generally of the form:
$$
y'(t)=P(t)y+g(t)
$$
- The solution is of the form:
$$
y=y_{c}+y_{p}
$$
- Particular and complementary solution 

- There is a method similar to method of variation of parameters used here to find the particular solution
	- Method of undetermined coefficients is a too lengthy method to solve so it is not used although it is possible. 

**Method of variation of parameters**
$$
\begin{array}{c}
\mathbf{y'}=P(t)\mathbf{y}+\mathbf{g}(t) \\
y(t_{0})=y_{0} \\ \displaystyle \large \boxed{
y(t)=\Psi(t)\Psi^{-1}(t_{0}) \mathbf{y_{0}} + \Psi(t) \int _{t_{0}}^{t} \Psi^{-1}g(s) \, ds} 
\end{array}
$$
